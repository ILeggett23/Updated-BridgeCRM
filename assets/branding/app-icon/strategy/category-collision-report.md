# Category-collision report

## Category baseline

The audited CRM field overuses blue trust palettes, orange automation palettes,
green growth palettes, initials, clouds, sprockets, link chains, node graphs,
people silhouettes, handshakes, chat bubbles, arrows, and centered symmetric
geometry. Personal CRMs add circles, address-book cues, contact cards, and
wordmark-led identities.

Sources and comparison set are documented in
`docs/brand/icon-strategy.md`. No competitor artwork is included in the master
assets or used as construction geometry.

## Territory collisions

### Open Span

Avoids letters, people, nodes, chat, charts, and pipelines. A generic arch is a
known broad-form risk, but the unequal support lengths and forward-shifted break
are not a common CRM composition. Deep forest and warm paper also avoid the
category's dominant bright-blue field. **Risk: low to moderate.**

### Held Thread

Avoids feature symbols and remains warm and human, but circular strokes are
common in productivity, sync, loading, and social products. The inward clasp
helps but does not fully remove a C or refresh reading. **Risk: moderate.**

### Memory Fold

Avoids CRM clichés, yet the clipped container and internal reveal overlap with
document, package, storage, camera, and crypto visual territory. **Risk:
moderate.**

### Second Beat

The unequal rhythm is strategically relevant, but the ring-plus-dot silhouette
can collide with magnifiers, cameras, accessibility symbols, social graphs, and
two-person abstractions. **Risk: moderate to high.**

### Relay Cut

The diagonal handoff is specific and works in one color. Two staggered tiles,
however, are common in workflow, automation, collaboration, and app-switching
brands. **Risk: moderate.**

## Final check

Open Span was selected because its collision risk stayed lowest while its
meaning remained product-specific. The remaining legal/trademark check should
search arch, doorway, hook, and broken-pillar marks outside CRM as well as
inside it before a formal registration filing.
